Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Raydius Labs, Inc. ("Raydius Labs," "we," "us") collects through this website and the apps and services we operate, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: details you enter when you create a profile or use a feature, such as your name, email address, photos, and the answers or interests you choose to share.
  • Sign-in information: if you sign in with a third-party account such as Facebook or Google, we receive the basic profile details that account shares with us, for example your name and email address. We do not receive your password.
  • Device and usage information: your device type, browser, approximate location when you give permission, and how you use our services.
  • Messages to us: anything you send us when you contact us.

How we use information

  • To provide, operate and improve our services.
  • To let you use features you choose, and to share information with other people only as you direct inside the service.
  • To keep our services safe, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To respond to you and to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

How we share information

We share information only with service providers who help us run our services (for example hosting and email providers) and who are bound to protect it, when the law requires it, or with your direction. If our company is ever part of a merger or sale, information may transfer to the new owner under this policy.

Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, then delete it or remove what identifies you. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see below).

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. To delete your data, follow the steps on our Data Deletion page. Residents of California and other states may have additional rights under local law, and we honor those requests.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
